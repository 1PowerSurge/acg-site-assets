What to Know Before Ordering a Banner

February 19, 2026

Ordering a banner should be straightforward, but it is a print category where mistakes are expensive and not always visible until the banner is hanging at your event. A banner ordered at the wrong size, without hemmed edges, or from a low-resolution file can mean a wasted order and a last-minute scramble. Understanding the key decisions before you place the order — material, size, finishing, and file requirements — means you get it right the first time.

Banner Materials and When to Use Each

Vinyl is the standard outdoor banner material — durable, weather-resistant, and reliable for most storefront, event, and parking lot applications. For trade shows and indoor displays, fabric banners are lighter and have a more polished look when hung properly. Mesh banners are designed for windy outdoor environments: the perforated surface lets air pass through, reducing stress on the banner and its mounting structure. If you are attaching a banner to a fence or chain-link at an outdoor event, mesh is worth considering over solid vinyl. Confirm your material choice before you design the file, since vinyl and fabric can reproduce color slightly differently.

Size Guide for Common Applications

For table displays, a 2 x 4 foot banner is standard. Storefront signage typically runs 3 x 6 feet — readable from the street and compatible with most standard mounting setups. Event banners at 4 x 8 feet are large enough to anchor a venue entrance and still manageable to transport and install with two people. When in doubt, measure the actual space where the banner will hang before placing your order.

Finishing: Hemming and Grommets

Two finishing specifications are essential for any banner that will be installed or handled repeatedly. Hemming means the edges of the banner are folded and welded or sewn, creating a reinforced border that prevents fraying and tearing under wind load. Always request hemmed edges — an unhemmed outdoor banner may not survive a weekend event intact.

Grommets are the metal rings inserted through the hemmed edges for hanging. Standard placement is every 2 feet around the perimeter, which distributes load evenly and prevents sagging. If your installation requires grommets in non-standard positions, specify the exact placement when you order. Most printers can accommodate custom grommet placement at no extra cost if you communicate it upfront.

File Requirements and Common Mistakes

Banners are viewed from a distance, so 100dpi at final print size is the minimum acceptable resolution. Set up your file at the actual banner dimensions — not at a reduced size. Use CMYK color mode and export as PDF or TIFF. Set your bleed to 0.5 inches to accommodate the hem fold. The most common ordering mistakes are uploading a low-resolution file, forgetting to specify indoor versus outdoor material, ordering the wrong size without measuring, and omitting the bleed so the background color does not extend into the hem area.
